EVIDENCE ID: H-01
TYPE: SIMULATED-CONTROLLED
PURPOSE: Visitor-area information exposure assessment
DATA: FICTIONAL

SCENARIO
--------
A staged visitor was placed only in the area considered accessible to visitors.
No restricted-area entry, deception of real staff or unauthorized device access
was performed.

OBSERVED IN THE TRAINING SCENARIO
---------------------------------
The visitor-accessible area exposed contextual information including:
- meeting-room names;
- a project reference;
- supplier context;
- normal visitor/reception process behavior.

SUPPORTED CONCLUSION
--------------------
A visitor can obtain internal contextual information useful for threat modeling.

NOT SUPPORTED
-------------
- credential theft;
- workstation access;
- restricted-area access;
- network compromise;
- real employee deception.
