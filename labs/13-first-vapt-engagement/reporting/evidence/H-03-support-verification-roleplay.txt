EVIDENCE ID: H-03
TYPE: SIMULATED-CONTROLLED
PURPOSE: Evaluate identity-verification strength in a fictional support recovery flow
DATA: FICTIONAL

SAFETY
------
No real employee was contacted.
No real account was reset.
No real personal data or credentials were used.

SCENARIO
--------
A role-play request used only contextual information available inside the training
discovery pack.

OBSERVED RESULT
---------------
The simulated support workflow accepted contextual organizational knowledge as
sufficient to advance further than intended before an independent verification
factor was required.

SUPPORTED CONCLUSION
--------------------
The simulated recovery workflow relied too heavily on familiarity/context and
not enough on mandatory independent identity verification.

NOT SUPPORTED
-------------
- real account takeover;
- real password reset;
- MFA bypass;
- production help-desk compromise;
- real social-engineering success.
