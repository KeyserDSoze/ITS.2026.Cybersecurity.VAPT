UMBRAMARKET S.R.L. — FORMATIVE SCOPE & RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

AUTHORIZED TARGETS

- shop.umbramarket.lab
- api.umbramarket.lab

DISCOVERED BUT NOT AUTHORIZED UNLESS THE INSTRUCTOR EXPANDS SCOPE

- any other hostname
- any other IP

TEST ACCOUNTS

alice.demo@example.lab
Role: customer

bob.demo@example.lab
Role: customer

The passwords are provided by the instructor as fictional lab credentials.

PERMITTED

- normal browsing;
- inspection of requests and responses;
- non-destructive parameter changes;
- comparison between the two provided accounts;
- scanner output supplied by the exercise;
- minimum proof needed to demonstrate an access-control problem.

NOT PERMITTED

- denial of service;
- brute force;
- password spraying;
- testing third-party services;
- modifying or deleting fictional customer records;
- persistence;
- collecting more customer records after the first sufficient proof;
- testing newly discovered hosts without explicit scope confirmation.

STOP CONDITION

If cross-user access to a protected customer object is demonstrated with one controlled example, stop that line of testing and preserve the evidence.

DELIVERABLES

Evidence pack, finding(s), remediation and short executive summary.
