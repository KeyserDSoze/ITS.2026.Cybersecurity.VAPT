UMBRAMARKET S.R.L. — FORMATIVE CLIENT BRIEF

UmbraMarket is preparing the public release of a fictional e-commerce platform.

The client asks:

"We want to understand whether an Internet user or a normal customer account could access information that should belong to another customer, or reach functionality that should not be exposed. We also want a short list of the risks that should be fixed before launch."

The client does NOT ask for maximum compromise. The goal is to identify and demonstrate meaningful risk with minimal impact.

Expected outputs:

- concise attack-surface inventory;
- validated findings only;
- evidence sufficient to reproduce the issue;
- prioritized remediation;
- executive summary for a non-technical stakeholder.

Before doing anything else, write what you still need to know about scope, credentials, limitations and stop conditions.
